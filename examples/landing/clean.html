Larkhill Tool Library
What you can borrowJoin

Borrow a drill instead of buying one

A lending library for tools in Larkhill. Members borrow ladders, sanders, wallpaper steamers and about 300 other tools for up to a week at a time.

Membership is £20 a year, or £5 if you get Universal Credit.

Opening hours

Thursday17:00 to 20:00
Saturday10:00 to 14:00

Back room of the community centre on Mill Lane. Step-free entrance from the car park.

What you can borrow

Around the house

Cordless drills, stud finders, a tile cutter, two wallpaper steamers and a carpet cleaner.

Garden

Hedge trimmers, a lawn scarifier, loppers, a log splitter and a wheelbarrow you can wheel home.

Bigger jobs

A 6-metre ladder, a mitre saw and a floor sander. These three need a 20-minute safety talk the first time.

How to join

  1. Come in during opening hours with proof of address.
  2. Pay the yearly fee by card or cash.
  3. Take a tool home the same day.
