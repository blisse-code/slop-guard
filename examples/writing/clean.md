# The tool library now opens on Saturdays

From 14 March the library on Mill Lane opens on Saturday mornings as well as Thursday evenings. Saturday hours are 10:00 to 14:00.

We added the second day because Thursday queues ran past closing time four weeks in a row, and three volunteers offered to cover a weekend shift.

Membership stays at £20 a year, or £5 if you get Universal Credit. You can join on either day with proof of address and take a tool home the same visit.

The floor sander and the 6-metre ladder still need a short safety talk the first time you borrow them. Ask whoever is on the desk.
